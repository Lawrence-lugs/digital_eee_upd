Instruction-format encoding table

This mirrors the R/I/I*/S/B/U/J summary on the reference sheet: most register and opcode fields stay aligned, while S/B/J rearrange immediate bits.

Bit numbers run from 31 at the left to 0 at the right. The highlighted row follows the current word whenever its opcode identifies a supported format.
Immediate range checks: I/S signed immediates [−2048, 2047]; shift amounts [0, 31]; B offsets [−4096, 4094] and even; J offsets [−1,048,576, 1,048,574] and even; U-type immu is a 20-bit field [0, 0xFFFFF].
